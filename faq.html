For job seekers
If you're looking for work
Money, matching, location, and what we promise
Is this really free? Will you ever ask for money?

Yes, completely free. We will never ask you for any fee — not for registration, not for matching, not for ‘premium access,’ not ever. If anyone claiming to represent Saarthi asks you for money, please report it to us on WhatsApp immediately. Our entire model is built on being free.

How long does matching take?

Usually 3-7 days, but it depends on what work you're looking for and where. Some matches happen the same day. Some take 2-3 weeks if there's no nearby demand for that skill. We'll be honest — if we can't find a match, we'll tell you.

What if you can't find me a job?

It happens. Sometimes there's no local demand for a specific skill, or your area isn't yet covered by our employer network. If that's the case, we'll tell you honestly — and may suggest free skill courses (in our Resources section) that could open more matches. We don't make false promises.

I'm not in Jhalawar. Can I still register?

Yes, please register — but be honest about your location. We're strongest in Jhalawar and nearby districts in Rajasthan and MP right now. Outside that, matching may be slow until we grow. We'd rather have you registered and waiting than turned away.

Do I need a CV, computer, or email to register?

No. You only need WhatsApp on any phone. The form is in Hindi and takes 2 minutes. If you want a CV later, we have a free Hindi CV template in our Resources section.

What kind of jobs do you have?

Right now mostly: shop & retail work (kirana, clothing, sweets), computer/office work (Tally, data entry, typing), labour/home/care work (driver, guard, cook, helper, construction). The mix grows as more employers join.

What if the employer doesn't pay me fairly?

Saarthi is just the bridge — we don't control employer behavior. But we ask employers to commit to fair pay and dignity, and we'd want to know if anyone treats you badly. If you have a bad experience, please tell us on WhatsApp. We'll stop sending people to that employer.

For employers
If you want to hire
What it costs and how trust works
Is hiring through Saarthi free for me too?

Yes. We charge employers nothing — no listing fee, no per-hire fee, nothing. We can do this because we're an NGO funded by our founders and small donations, not by transactions.

How do I know the candidates are reliable?

Saarthi doesn't do background checks — we connect, you decide. Treat our matches like a recommendation from a community contact. We do encourage candidates to register honestly, but you should always interview, check references locally, and trust your own judgment before hiring.

About Saarthi
Who we are and how we work
The people, the partnerships, the data
Who is behind Saarthi Trust?

A small team of alumni from IITs, IIMs, AIIMS and NLUs — most of us grew up in towns and villages and understand the gap between ability and opportunity. You can meet the team on our About page. We are registered as a Trust under the Indian Trusts Act.

Can my company partner with Saarthi?

Yes — we work with companies in three ways: (1) hiring matched candidates, (2) sponsoring skill workshops in rural schools, (3) CSR funding for specific programs. Write to us at contact@saarthitrust.org with what you have in mind.

How do you use the data I share?

Only for matching you with employers/seekers, and to message you on WhatsApp about your matches. We never sell or share your data with third parties. You can ask us to delete your data anytime by messaging us on WhatsApp.
